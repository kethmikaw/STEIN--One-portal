COMMON PROCESS

System overview

One portal manages submission, approvals and completion. Each process adds its own business rules.

Read the main path from top to bottomEach box names the responsible role
  1. 01 · Requester

    Choose a form

    Select one of the eight request types.

  2. 02 · Requester

    Complete and save

    Enter details, add items or evidence, and save a draft.

  3. 03 · System

    Validate the submission

    Check required fields, quantities, dates, duplicates and the approval route.

  4. 04 · Approver

    Review and decide

    Approve, return for correction or reject. Multiple signatories act in the agreed sequence.

  5. 05 · Operational team

    Carry out the approved work

    Issue goods, receive equipment, repair an asset, post leave, process payroll or hand off an appointment.

  6. 06 · Responsible team

    Confirm the actual outcome

    Record receipt, return, service test, posting, payment or appointment handoff.

  7. 07 · System

    Complete and retain

    Keep a searchable record with versions, decisions, handovers and timestamps.

Branches and exceptions

Validation fails

The requester corrects the fields before submission.

Returned for correction

The requester edits a new version and resubmits for affected approvals.

Rejected

Notify the requester and retain the decision; stop processing.

Partly completed

Keep the balance or unresolved work open until fulfilled or explicitly cancelled.

Approval and completion are separate: approved goods are not yet issued, approved leave is not yet posted, and an approved claim is not yet paid.

Every step retains the request reference, responsible person, timestamp and current version. Return and rejection decisions require a reason.

Proposed workflow based on the Stein PRD · Business approval rules must be confirmed before implementation.
